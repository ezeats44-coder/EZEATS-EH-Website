# EZEATS EH Privacy Policy

**Effective September 15, 2026.**

EZEATS EH is operated by EZ-EATS EH LLC. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our food recommendation website at ezeats-eh.com. Contact us with privacy questions at ezeats44@gmail.com.

## Information you provide

**Account information.** If you create an account using email, Apple, or Google, our authentication provider, Clerk, processes the information needed to create and secure your account. This may include your email address, name, profile image, provider account identifier, and sign-in records. Apple may provide a private relay email address when you choose Hide My Email. Passwords and verification codes are handled by our authentication provider; our meal recommendation code does not read them.

Google sign-in requests basic identity information: your account identifier, email address, and basic profile. EZEATS EH uses this information to identify you, manage your account, and let you return to your saved preferences. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, calendar, or Google Drive files.

**Optional profile answers.** You can save food preferences such as diet, spice tolerance, favorite cuisines, ingredient dislikes, allergies, cooking time, budget, and eating habits. You can also choose to provide personal information such as your name, age range, gender, relationship status, student status, and pregnancy status. These questions are optional, and you can skip setup or leave individual answers blank.

Allergy and pregnancy answers can reveal sensitive health-related information. Saving your profile saves any such answers you choose to enter. Avoid entering information you do not want stored with your account. These answers are not posted publicly or shared with other users.

**Messages to us.** If you contact us, we receive the information you include in your message and your contact details.

**Recent meal picks.** When signed in, selecting “That’s the one” saves the meal identifier, a choice identifier, and the time with your account. We keep up to 20 recent confirmed picks to favor cuisines you have chosen and reduce recent repeats. These are choices within EZEATS, not verified purchases or meals eaten. Choices made before this feature launched are not reconstructed. You can remove this history using **Clear recent picks** on the personal meal picker. Guest choices are not saved to this history.

## Guest use and technical information

You can use the meal picker without an account. Guest meal choices are held in the page's memory and are not saved as an account profile. Closing or reloading the page resets those choices.

Guest use still involves ordinary website requests. Our hosting and authentication providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, request times, and security or error logs to deliver and protect the service. Authentication uses cookies or similar browser storage to manage sign-in sessions. Blocking required authentication storage may prevent sign-in from working.

## How we use information

We use account information to authenticate users and associate saved preferences with the correct account. Saved food preferences help filter and rank meals from our curated list. We also use information to operate the website, troubleshoot problems, protect accounts, and respond to requests.

Not every questionnaire answer currently affects recommendations. Personal demographic answers and pregnancy status are not used to rank meals or assess medical needs. Allergy screening is limited to our meal data and cannot verify ingredient brands or cross-contact. Recommendations are not medical advice or a guarantee that a meal is safe for an allergy.

The current recommendation feature runs in your browser. It does not send your answers to an external generative AI service. The current website does not include advertising pixels or a feature that sells your profile information.

## Service providers and information sharing

We use Clerk for authentication and storage of account-linked preferences, and Vercel for website hosting and server functions. Apple or Google also processes your sign-in when you choose its service. Account and profile information passes through the providers needed to deliver these features. Authorized EZEATS personnel and service providers may access information as needed to operate, support, or protect the service; “private to your account” does not mean inaccessible to the operator or its providers.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their services are also subject to their respective privacy notices: [Clerk](https://clerk.com/legal/privacy), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to address fraud, abuse, or threats to the security of the service.

## Storage, retention, and your choices

Profile answers and up to 20 recent meal picks are stored as account-linked private metadata in Clerk. The website's profile service requires an authenticated session before reading, changing, or deleting those answers or picks. History-based ranking uses picks from the last 90 days; stored picks remain until displaced by newer picks, cleared, or the account is deleted. Clearing recent picks resets history-based personalization without changing your stated preferences. Deleting preferences alone does not clear recent picks. Connections use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Saved preferences remain associated with your account until you change or delete them or delete your account. In Settings, you can clear individual answers, save updated preferences, or select **Delete saved preferences**. Deleting preferences does not delete your sign-in account. **Manage account** provides account controls, including account deletion. Removing the connection in Apple or Google alone does not necessarily delete your EZEATS account or saved profile.

You can also contact ezeats44@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner. Deletion from the active application does not necessarily immediately remove security logs or provider backups; their retention depends on operational requirements and provider practices.

## Age requirement

EZEATS accounts are available to people age 13 and older. After your first sign-in, we ask you to confirm this once before beginning personalized setup or accessing saved preferences. We save your age-eligibility confirmation and its date with your account so you do not need to repeat it when signing in again or using another device. A previously saved confirmation of being 14 or older also meets this requirement. This is a self-declaration, not identity or age verification; we do not collect a date of birth for this check. The optional age-range question in Settings starts at 13–17 and is separate from eligibility confirmation.

If you believe someone under 13 has created an account, contact ezeats44@gmail.com so we can investigate and address the account and associated information. The guest meal picker does not require an account.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our information practices change and show the effective date on the published page. If we introduce materially different uses of account or profile information, we will provide notice and obtain consent where required before applying those uses.

# Contributor privacy and rights additions — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

Proposed wording for owner review before invitation-only beta activation. This is not the current website policy. The owner must approve the rights permission, retention period and removal process before real contributors use the beta.

## Invitation-only recipes

Selected existing EZEATS account holders may receive contributor access from an authorized owner. We use your account identifier and existing age-eligibility confirmation to manage access. An owner may look up an existing verified email address through our authentication service; we record the account identifier, who invited it, invitation dates, expiration and revocation. Invitations do not create an account or send an email in this beta.

We store recipe drafts, ingredients, instructions, attribution, source and copyright statements, dietary/allergen claims, revision history and moderation decisions. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or private information about other people in a recipe. Your profile answers are not copied into recipe records. Owners can see the submitting account privately to administer the beta.

## Private and public information

Unpublished recipes are accessible to the submitting contributor and authorized EZEATS owners. Owners can review source information, rights statements, history and safety claims. Owner decision notes remain private to owners; a separate explanation may be shared with the submitting contributor when changes are requested or a recipe is rejected. We do not publish the contributor invitation list, account identifiers or private moderation records.

If approved and published, the recipe and your chosen public author attribution can be read by anyone. Choose an attribution name you are comfortable making public. Approved source credit may also be public. Dietary and allergy labels remain claims, not assurances of safety. Editorial approval is not kitchen testing, medical advice or a cross-contact guarantee. Check ingredients, package labels, substitutions and safe food handling.

## Rights and removal

You must confirm that your recipe is original or that you have permission to submit it, identifying the source and permission basis when applicable. The proposed beta permission allows EZEATS to retain, review and display approved recipe revisions with attribution, including after sign-in account deletion, until a removal decision. This does not transfer copyright or grant other users a reuse license. The exact permission and consent wording must be approved before activation; a checkbox alone is not a substitute for a reviewed contributor agreement.

You may withdraw an unpublished editable draft from the contributor workspace. Ask EZEATS to review removal of pending or published content, rights concerns, attribution changes or deletion requests using the contact method in the main privacy policy. Withdrawal removes public availability where applicable but preserves private revision and moderation history. We must review requests involving another rights holder or legal record obligations individually.

## Account deletion and retention

Deleting your sign-in account ends access; it does not automatically delete published recipes. Owners can confirm account deletion through the authentication service, revoke contributor access and review pending submissions. Pending recipes stay private while that review occurs. Previously published recipes remain attributed and published until an owner removes them under the approved removal policy. Account deletion does not create new publication permission.

Proposed beta operating policy: retain active recipes and the revisions needed to support them while they are used. Review withdrawn/rejected/unpublished content for deletion or minimization within 90 days of withdrawal, account-deletion reconciliation or a valid deletion request, subject to documented rights/dispute needs. Review associated private audit records for minimization after 12 months unless needed for an active publication or documented dispute. These are proposed manual review deadlines, not implemented automatic erasure. Do not publish these periods until a responsible operator and a tested erasure/minimization procedure are assigned.

Database backups may retain older copies until the configured backup retention expires. Restoring a backup must reapply recorded removal/deletion decisions before serving content. The actual backup retention period must be verified for the approved hosting plan and stated before activation; no backup period is asserted here. This implementation does not purge immutable records or backups automatically.

## Service providers and changes

The beta uses EZEATS's existing authentication and database/hosting services to process account identity and recipe records. No new advertising, analytics, email distribution or external recipe provider is introduced. Image uploads and public recipe submissions remain unavailable. We will update the live policy and contributor notice only after the beta and these additions are approved.
